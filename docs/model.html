Recommendations

Default for full scans: Codex gpt-6.1-sol at xhigh effort.

On every repository where it ran next to a Claude model on the same code it found the largest severity-weighted share of issues (gocql 0.76 vs Fable 0.43; seastar 0.81 vs Opus 0.30; scylla-rust-driver 0.59 vs Fable 0.55), at $0.27–0.52 per 100KB — about a seventh of Fable's price on the same 2.7MB of Rust. Auto-triage confirmed ≥ 0.99 of its findings, but that triage was also done by Codex and rejected almost nothing (4 false positives in 1,623 Codex verdicts), so treat that figure as an upper bound.

Best Claude reviewer: Opus 5.5 at 20KB chunks.

Found all 7 known bugs on the gocql benchmark at $1.19/100KB and topped the calibration sample of the private Go project (0.74). Full scans cost $0.38–0.73/100KB with 0.81–1.00 precision after triage. Use it when you want a Claude-only pipeline or as the second opinion next to Codex.

Fable 5.2: strongest on small samples, most expensive at scale.

Best quality on the seastar and gocql calibration samples (0.65, 0.49), but $1.93/100KB on the full scylla-rust-driver scan — about seven times Codex and five times Opus on the same repository. Worth it for a focused scan of a critical subsystem (--only), not for a whole large repo.

Sonnet 5.5: cheap but inconsistent. Haiku 5.5: don't use for review.

Sonnet ranged from 0.68 (private Go) to 0.09 (seastar C++) on calibration samples, and found 4 of 7 known gocql bugs. Haiku found 2 of 7, scored 0.00–0.32 and had rounds fail because reviewers left files unsubmitted.

For critical repos, run two models side by side.

Overlap between models is small: of the issues Codex and Fable found on gocql only 25 were found by both, on scylla-rust-driver only 12. In the effort study below, Codex xhigh alone found 0.40 of the confirmed issues and 0.66 together with Opus max. start --profile codex-default,opus-default runs both on the same commit and plan; compare shows what only one found.

Chunk size

Let init calibrate it — and expect 20–40KB.

Review quality falls off sharply once a reviewer gets too much code at once. Across the 12 calibration curves below, the best-scoring size was 20KB or 40KB in 9, and in 8 quality fell by a quarter or more one step past it (Fable on seastar: 0.78 at 40KB → 0.39 at 60KB). Only one run peaked above 60KB: Opus on cpp-rs-driver, at 90KB. Calibration costs $1–5 per model for Sonnet and Opus, $7–15 for Fable, and is reused by every later scan.
