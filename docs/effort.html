Recall = share of the 217 confirmed issues all ten runs found together on nodejs-rs-driver (TypeScript + Rust). Effort was measured on that one repository; the trend (recall up, precision flat) is what to expect elsewhere, not the exact numbers.

Findings

Codex: use xhigh. Use high on a tight budget. Skip ultra.

Recall rises steadily with effort up to xhigh: 0.17 at low ($2.41), 0.23 medium, 0.31 high ($4.28), 0.40 xhigh ($7.49). max adds almost nothing (0.41 for $8.54), and ultra found less (0.36) for more money ($9.44). xhigh costs $0.75 per 100KB and about $0.10 per confirmed bug.

Opus: low, medium and high find about the same. max costs five times as much for twice the recall.

Opus scored 0.12, 0.15 and 0.17 at low, medium and high ($7.72 → $11.93), so if you scan with Opus, low or medium is enough. max doubled recall to 0.35 but cost $58.61 ($5.89/100KB, 1.4M output tokens), eight times Codex xhigh, which found more.

Effort does not change the false-positive rate.

Precision stayed at 0.94–0.99 at every level of both models: higher effort finds more real bugs, not cleaner ones. Codex reports most issues as high and Opus rates them lower; after triage both land mostly on medium (see the severity table).

A second run beats a bigger one.

No single run found more than 41% of the confirmed issues. Codex high + xhigh together reach 0.49 for $11.77, more than any single run at any price. Codex xhigh + Opus max reach 0.66, and all six Codex runs together reach 0.76 for $35. For critical code, run xhigh plus one different configuration rather than going to max/ultra.

One repository, one run per level, and a Codex judge (which may favour Codex findings), so treat differences under ~0.05 as noise.
